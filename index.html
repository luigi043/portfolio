About Me

I'm a computer engineer with a B.Sc. from IPBeja. My work spans full-stack web applications, native Android development with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, and embedded systems built with Arduino and Raspberry Pi.

I have delivered projects across modern and legacy stacks: a Spring Boot and React vehicle platform, privacy-first health applications, an insurance workflow system on .NET and WCF, and custom control systems for a BMW E36. The common thread is practical engineering: understand the constraints, choose the right architecture, and make the result straightforward to use.

I do my best work with end-to-end ownership: data modelling, backend services, frontend experience, testing, and deployment. That broad view helps me make decisions that work across the whole system instead of optimising one layer in isolation.

My experience includes internships, independent client delivery, and product-focused personal work. I also hold certifications in Generative AI for Software Developers (IBM), UX Design (Google), and .NET full-stack development.

Before moving fully into software, I worked in hospitality across Portugal. That experience strengthened the communication, composure, and customer awareness I now bring to technical teams and client work.
